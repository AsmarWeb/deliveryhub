# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

DeliveryHub aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome different viewpoints.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not individuals.
- Help maintain a productive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

For serious issues, contact the project maintainers privately using the contact information available through the project profile.
